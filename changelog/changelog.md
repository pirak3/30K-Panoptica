  - `[Macharius Heavy Tank Squadron]`: sponson Heavy Stubbers may be exchanged for Heavy Bolters or Heavy Flamers

  - `[Primaris-Lightning Strike Fighter]`: Added Multi-Lasers, EM-Storm Charges, Reaper Autocannons, Cyclone Missile Launcher, Phosphex Cluster Bombs (with a Siege Breaker), Hull Predator Cannon and Battle Servitor Control
  - `[Tarantula Sentry Gun Battery]`: Added six new weapon variants, deployment options and the Hyperios Command Platform
  - `[Javelin Squadron]`, `[Proteus Land Speeder Squadron]`: one model may take a Legion Vexilla
  - `[Terminator Indomitus Squad]`: Added Plasma Blaster / Plasma Cannon (shared 1 per 5 heavy allowance), Havoc Launcher (1 per 5), Volkite Charger and Lightning Claw options
  - `Consul`: `Legiones Consularis` options other than Forge Lord, Siege Breaker and Mortificator are hidden for `[Centurion, Saturnine]`
  - `[Praetor, Cataphractii]`, `[Praetor, Tartaros]`: Master-crafted (+10) now available to all legions
  - `[Command Squad]`, `[Command Squad, Cataphractii/Tartaros/Indomitus]`: may take `[Land Raider Tartaros]` as Dedicated Transport
  - `[Master of Armour]`: not required under Armoured Spearhead when a `[Land Raider Dionysus]` is taken
  - `[Cassian Dracos]`: Warlord restriction now also covers the new Indomitus/Saturnine HQs and both new Land Raiders
  - Siege Breaker name conditions also accept the Pater Consularis Siege Breaker
  - `[Castra Ferrum Dreadnought Talon]`: Added Frag Launchers, Volkite Culverin / Gravis Siege Drill / Seismic Hammer swaps, in-built Plasma Blaster / Graviton Gun and Hunter-Killer Missiles
  - `[Techmarine Covenant]`: may take up to 3 `[Servo-Automata Maniple]` as Retinue (one per Techmarine)
  - `[Charnabal Weapon]`: Added `[Charnabal Longsword]`
  - `[Angel's Wrath]`: units that may take a Land Raider Proteus Carrier may take a `[Caestus Assault Ram]` instead
  - `[Sky-Hunter Phalanx]`: `[Sky-Seeker Squadron]` loses Support Squad and may fill Compulsory Troops
  - `[Pride of the Legion]`: `[Assault Veteran Squad]` may be Troops (Line when Compulsory); `[Terminator Saturnine Squad]` may be Troops or Heavy Support
  - `[Fury of the Ancients]`: `[Castra Ferrum Dreadnought Talon]` may be (Compulsory) Troops with Line; `[Contemptor-Cortus Dreadnought Talon]` may be Troops
  - `[Dark Angels]`: Heavy Bolters, Twin-linked Heavy Bolters, Gravis Bolt Cannons and Gravis Heavy Bolter Batteries may be upgraded to Xenobane Cannons (+2/+2/+5/+5 per weapon)
  - `[Space Wolves]`: Characters may exchange a Bolt Pistol for a Helfrost Pistol (+20)
  - `[Armoury of the Space Wolves]`: now lists four Space Wolves consuls (adds Priest of Iron)
  - `[Huscarl Squad]`: only one Huscarl Squad or Huscarl Indomitus Terminator Squad per army
  - `[Deliverers Squad]`: only one Deliverers Squad or Deliverers Tartaros Squad per army
  - `[Praetor]`, `[Praetor, Cataphractii/Indomitus/Tartaros]`: an Iron Hands Iron-Father Warlord may take `[The Keys of Hel]` (+30); Ferrus Manus cannot be taken alongside it
  - `[Medusan Immortal Squad]`: 1 in 5 Immortals may take a Power Weapon (+10)
  - `[Iron Hands]`: Characters may exchange a Power Weapon for a `[Medusan Zweihander]` (+10), including the `[Morlock Terminator Squad]` Augmentor
  - Armoured Spearhead: the Master of Armour requirement now uses a new `Master of Armour:` category (carried by `[Master of Armour]`, `[Land Raider Dionysus]` and `[Castrmen Orth]`)
  - The Gates of Hel: `[Lord Crius]` must be the Warlord when no Praetor has `[The Keys of Hel]`
  - `[Keys of Hel]` rule now lives once in Legiones Astartes (shared by the Praetor upgrade and Lord Crius)
  - Destroyer Company: Rad Grenades (+10) for Infantry/Cavalry Characters and Rad Missiles (+5 per model) for Missile Launchers; `[Heavy Destroyer Squad]` Teleportation Transponders also unlock with it
  - Breacher Spearhead: `[Tactical Support Squad]` and `[Veteran Squad]` may take Boarding Shields (Heavy)
  - Rite requirements enforced: Destroyer Company needs a Siege Breaker or Moritat (new `Moritat:` category); Tactica Obliqua needs 2 `[Tactical Squad]`, a `[Heavy Support Squad]` and an `[Assault Squad]` or `[Despoiler Squad]`. Breacher Heavy majority and Storm-Lance Warlord bike are marked as manual checks
  - `[Centurion]`, `[Centurion, Cataphractii/Tartaros/Indomitus]`: Lanistus consul swaps (Caedere weapons, banned bikes/jump packs/bolters/combi-weapons)
  - `[Tactical Squad]`, `[Despoiler Squad]`: Added `[Savage Arsenal]` upgrade (one unit per army, needs a Lanistus); unlocks Caedere weapon swaps for that unit
  - `[Champion]` consul: Never Back Down now links the shared rule

  - `[Gravis Missile Launcher]`: Added `Rad` profile (unlocked with Rad Missiles under Destroyer Company, +5 once per model)
